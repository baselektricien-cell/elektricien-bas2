Dutch electrical terms, explained

  • Groepenkast / meterkast: the fuse box or consumer unit
  • Aardlekschakelaar: the residual current device (RCD) that trips when there is a leak to earth
  • Groep: a circuit
  • Storing / kortsluiting: a fault / short circuit

My power went out. What should I do?

Don't touch damaged cables or scorched sockets. Check which circuit or RCD has tripped in the fuse box, unplug appliances on that circuit and switch it back on. If it trips again, leave it off and call 06 1171 6662.
